# Complete settings view audit

Audit date: 6 November 2026. Baseline: `d936a4ec54b2860df73666e91046267b80e6db5c`. Branch: `chore/settings-complete-view-audit`.

**No, every settings view does not earn 9 in every category.** Of 136 distinct route/tab views, **74 have at least one category below 9** and 62 meet the seven-category target in the inspected fixture states. The standard forms are substantially more consistent than the custom editors. Broadcast, Radar, Profiles actions, Fuel/Input layout editors and Advanced Telemetry are the largest exceptions. The earlier representative review remains valid as a historical assessment of its stated scope; it cannot establish scores for these previously unreviewed surfaces.

This is an independent, single-reviewer aesthetic audit. Scores are heuristic judgements, not participant results, task success rates, a validated questionnaire or accessibility certification. Deterministic interaction and contrast observations are reported separately. No production UI, logo, typography, telemetry, preferences, profile/storage/IPC semantics or startup window dimensions were changed.

## Scoring method

The unchanged categories and weights are H visual hierarchy 20%, T typography/numerals 20%, S spacing/composition 15%, C colour/contrast 15%, F consistency/finish 10%, R restraint 10%, I identity/character 10%. Original anchors remain: 1–2 incoherent, 3–4 dominant weaknesses, 5–6 competent but uneven, 7–8 polished with identifiable compromises, 9–10 exceptional execution across the reviewed states and contexts. Weighted totals are arithmetic rounded to one decimal, not measurement precision. There is no combined whole-app or overlay score.

Each route/tab vector reflects its own render and applicable captured expanded/error/editor states, including the weakest required size. A medium view cannot average away compact clipping. Shared controls can share a rating rationale after their enclosing route has been rendered. No score was raised to meet a target. A blank Findings cell means no below-target aesthetic discrepancy was identified in that view's inspected states; it does not certify every possible interaction or configuration. Native blocked states are unscored.

General, standard Visibility panels, clean option forms and the refined Header/Footer subgrids supply the strongest current examples: clear 24px page titles, readable settings-owned Segoe UI, contextual secondary captions, consistent field boundaries, restrained surfaces and faithful red/logo identity. Long forms are not inherently bad. Deductions below concern competing emphasis, weak grouping, inconsistent roles and concrete loss of space or content.

## Aesthetic discrepancies and recommendations

P1 means content/action access is materially compromised at a required size. P2 means a repeatable visual or control-system inconsistency. P3 means local polish. These are audit priorities, not estimates from user research. Finding IDs are referenced by the per-view tables and external inventory.

### A01 — Broadcast responsive composition, P1

All five module routes fall below target. At 808×656, Tower's preview begins at y=656 with height 422.22px, while its clipping ancestor ends at y=640 and has `overflow:hidden`. The preview is entirely outside the visible region. The module rail, global transition explanation, URL and module header leave a very short inner form viewport. Scrolling that form does not bring the preview into view. The wide Tower capture also shows its focus-driver card extending left from the preview into the settings column. The inspected preview state therefore needs containment as well as responsive placement.

Recommend one deliberate scroll owner, a compact module chooser/rail treatment and a preview region that remains reachable in normal flow. Contain the preview's own positioned content within that region. Preserve its actual rendering and configuration semantics. Increasing the startup window size is not an authorized remedy.

Evidence: [compact Tower](C:/Users/tarik/.t3/review-media/settings-complete-view-audit/screens/view-broadcast-page-808x656-top.png), [wide Tower](C:/Users/tarik/.t3/review-media/settings-complete-view-audit/screens/view-broadcast-page-1920x1080-top.png), other four module IDs in the score table; source `sections/BroadcastGroupSettings.tsx` and `BroadcastPreview.tsx`.

With two profiles at compact size, the second Actions menu opens below its row and beyond the viewport. Rename/Delete are below the visible edge; a normal pointer click on Delete times out as outside the viewport. Recommend viewport collision handling, correct repositioning/closure on scroll, a bounded menu, scoped settings typography/tokens and one popup surface treatment. Retain proper button/group semantics and focus restoration. Test the final row at compact size with a pointer and keyboard.
